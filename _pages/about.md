My name is Weiyong Xu (徐卫勇 in Chinese). I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Psychology, University of Jyväskylä. I currently work on the Research Council of Finland-funded Trajectory project, led by Prof. Tiina Parviainen. From 2022 to 2025, I led the Research Council of Finland-funded postdoctoral project [“The reorganization of memory representations during rest and sleep”](https://converis.jyu.fi/converis/portal/detail/Project/156840914?lang=en_GB).

In 2020, I completed my PhD in cognitive neuroscience at the University of Jyväskylä, supervised by Prof. [Jarmo Hämäläinen](https://www.jyu.fi/fi/henkilot/jarmo-hamalainen). My doctoral research used magnetoencephalography (MEG) to investigate brain activity changes during audiovisual learning and integration in reading. During my doctoral training, I also worked in the European [ChildBrain](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/641652) network (2015–2019).

Before starting my PhD in Finland, I received my master’s and bachelor’s degrees in biomedical engineering from Xi’an Jiaotong University, China. My master’s thesis examined functional connectivity and complex networks in the ageing brain using electroencephalography (EEG).
